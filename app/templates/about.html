About OralScan AI

Explainable Artificial Intelligence for early oral pre-cancer and cancer triage screening.

Project Mission & Clinical Context

AI in Healthcare

Oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) is frequently preceded by clinically identifiable Oral Potentially Malignant Disorders (OPMD), including oral submucous fibrosis (OSMF) and leukoplakia. In resource-limited settings, late diagnosis dramatically reduces 5-year survival rates. OralScan AI provides rapid, on-device risk stratification of smartphone intraoral photographs into 4 actionable triage tiers.

Triage Framework
  • T0 Normal: Routine recall
  • T1 Variation from normal: Short-interval review
  • T2 OPMD (incl. OSMF): Refer to oral-medicine specialist
  • T3 Oral cancer: URGENT referral
Explainable AI (XAI) Suite
  • Grad-CAM: Gradient-weighted activations at the final LayerNorm.
  • Attention Rollout: 12-layer multi-head self-attention product with raw border fidelity.
  • LIME: Top 5 positive superpixel outlines with black padding excluded.

Privacy & Local Processing

Zero Cloud Dependency

All model inference, specular highlight inpainting, Shades-of-Gray colour constancy, and explainability algorithms execute locally on CPU. No patient images are saved to disk, and no third-party APIs (OpenAI, Gemini, Hugging Face) are called.
