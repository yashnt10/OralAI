Model & Training Performance

Transparent validation metrics, patient-grouped split validation, loss curves, and confusion matrix for the fine-tuned ViT-B/16 oral pre-cancer screening model.

Runs locally · no external AI APIs

All image inference and explainability heatmaps (Grad-CAM, Attention Rollout, LIME) are computed locally on-device using the fine-tuned ViT-B/16 checkpoint. Zero third-party AI APIs (no OpenAI, Gemini, or Hugging Face Inference endpoints) are used — all processing stays entirely on your local machine.
